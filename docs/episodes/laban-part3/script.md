# Laban — Part 3 script

Labanotation is written on a vertical staff and read from the bottom upward. Where a symbol sits tells
you which part of the body is moving. Its shape can indicate direction. Its shading can indicate level.
Its length communicates duration.

In principle, movement becomes something another person can read and reconstruct long after the
original dancer has left the room.

GRAPHIC: Stick figure moves while original Labanotation-style symbols appear beside it. Build from
documented principles rather than copying a published score.

Requested: best animations, text where required, split screens, masking & rotoscoping, whip pan,
speed ramping.
