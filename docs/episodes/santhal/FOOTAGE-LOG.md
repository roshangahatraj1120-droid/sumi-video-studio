# Footage log: Santhal dance gathering, Kechana (Jhapa)

These are 91 Drive files: 90 video clips and 1 photo, about 27 minutes in total, all shot on 25 Oct. Two files were uploaded twice (IMG_0274, IMG_0275), so I dropped the extra copies.

The location comes from a banner (IMG_6555): *सन्थाली युथ क्लब, कचनकवल-४, हरियाली टोल, केचना (झापा) नेपाल* ("Santhali Youth Club, Kachankawal-4, Hariyali Tol, Kechana, Jhapa"). A green banner in IMG_0307 starts with "आदिवासी…" (Adivasi). The judges' table holds trophies (IMG_6595/6596), so this looks like a group dance competition or rally.

There are two cameras:
- **A cam (IMG_65xx/66xx):** landscape, 4K in the morning and 1080p in the later clips.
- **B cam (IMG_02xx/03xx):** 4K, golden-hour light. Nine of these clips are vertical: 0274, 0275, 0284, 0285, 0293, 0299, 0300, 0301, 0302, 0303, 0304, 0306. These are ready-made Shorts.

Audio is loud street, drum and crowd sound throughout, with no quiet gaps. The interviews will need noise reduction and subtitles.
