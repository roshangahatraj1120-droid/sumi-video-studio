# Rudolf Laban: full-script SFX sheet (CapCut)

Every line of the script, start to finish, with the clip that covers it and the sound to add, or **—**
for nothing. Minimal on purpose: **43 drops in 10 minutes** (plus 4 optional), mostly where the picture already hits.
Everything else is your voice and the music.

## Step 1: build your sound kit once

Find these once in CapCut (**Audio → Sound effects**), then **reuse the exact same file every time**.
Repeating sounds is what makes it feel designed.

| Code | CapCut search | Pick one that is… | Volume | Fade |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **WHOOSH** | `whoosh` | fast and airy, about 0.5s | 35% | none |
| **SWOOSH** | `swoosh` | slow and soft, 1–2s | 30% | none |
| **IMPACT** | `cinematic impact` | low and soft, a short tail, not a punch | 40% | 0.2s out |
| **BOOM** | `cinematic boom` | deep, with a long tail | 50% | 1s out |
| **GLITCH** | `glitch` | short and digital, under 0.5s | 30% | none |
| **WIND** | `wind gust` | a soft gust or sand swirl, 2–3s | 25% | 0.5s in and out |
| **SHUTTER** | `camera shutter` | an old mechanical shutter | 30% | none |
| **TYPE** | `typewriter` | a single key press | 30% | none |

One-time sounds: `paper tear`, `data` (or `computer beep`), `riser`, `train station`, `clock ticking`,
`record scratch`, `stamp`, `projector`, `scan`, and optionally `factory ambience`.

**Three sounds come back on purpose, so the video rhymes with itself:**
- **GLITCH** = *"stranger"* (the intro, then again in the Pentagon section).
- **WIND** = *movement disappearing* (the intro, Part 3, the conclusion).
- **SHUTTER** = *someone being observed* (the Movement Pattern Analysis section).

## Step 2: how to drop each sound
1. Put all SFX on **one audio track** under your voice.
2. Click the clip in the timeline and put the playhead on its first frame. Move forward by the
   **Time** shown below, then drop the sound there. For impacts and booms, line up the **peak** with that
   frame, not the start of the file.
3. Never louder than your voice. If you can consciously hear it over a sentence, turn it down.
4. **Monetization:** if CapCut shows a *Commercial use* filter or label, only pick sounds marked for it.
   If unsure, get the same search word from **YouTube Studio → Audio Library → Sound effects**.

## Quick count
- Opening 11 · Architect 5 · Nazi Germany 5 · Factory 2 (+1 optional) · Preserve & own 2 (+1 optional) ·
  MPA 12 · Other life 2 · Conclusion 4 (+1 optional) · CTA 0 (+1 optional)
- That's **43 drops using 17 different sounds**, plus 4 optional. Most sections have **long stretches
  with no SFX at all**, and that's the point.

## Leave out
- No SFX under the host talking on camera.
- No sound on every cut or every word, and no whooshes on Part 3's whip pans or the Part 8b cuts.
- Don't swap in a new whoosh "for variety". The same 2 whooshes, 1 impact and 1 boom all video long is what
  makes it sound professional.
