## About the symbols
Clip 08 doesn't copy any published score. It follows the documented principles:
- The staff reads bottom to top.
- The centre line divides left from right.
- Leg and support columns sit next to the centre line; arm columns sit outside the staff.
- Shape shows direction: place, forward, diagonal.
- Shading shows level: striped = high, dot = middle, solid = low.
- Length shows duration.
- Small circles are hold signs between movements.

## Rotoscoping
The dancers in images 1–5 were cut out with an AI segmentation model, so text and arrows can sit behind them.
