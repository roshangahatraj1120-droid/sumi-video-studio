# Laban documentary — minimal SFX cue sheet (CapCut)

**Rule:** sound only on the big moments. About 25 cues in a 10-minute video, 2–3 per chapter. Everything
else is voice and music. **Times are from the start of each clip**, so put the playhead at the clip's
first frame, move it forward by the time shown, and drop the sound there.

## Where to get the sounds (monetization-safe)
- **YouTube Studio → Audio Library → Sound effects** tab (free, safe for monetized videos). Search words
  are given in each row.
- In CapCut, only use sounds you know are cleared for commercial use. The YouTube library is the safest.

## Volume (CapCut → select the sound → Volume)
| Type | Volume | Fade |
|---|---|---|
| Whoosh | 30–40% | none |
| Hit / impact | 40% | 0.2s fade-out |
| Low boom | 50% | 1s fade-out |
| Ticks, typewriter, shutter | 25–35% | none |
| Ambience (train, projector, wind) | 15–20% | 1s in and out |

Never louder than your voice. If you can't hear a word over a sound, lower the sound, not the voice.

## Also skip
- No SFX under the host talking.
- No sound on every cut or every word. Only the 25 above.
- No whoosh on whip pans that land on a quiet moment (Part 3's chain of whips works better silent with music).
