# Laban — Part 6 script: Britain and Industrial Rhythm

In Britain, Laban's work took an unexpected turn. During the Second World War, industrial production
was under enormous pressure.

With management consultant F. C. Lawrence, Laban developed an approach usually described as
Laban-Lawrence Industrial Rhythm. They studied how workers moved - not dancers, but people performing
repetitive industrial labor.

The idea was not simply 'move faster.' Laban was interested in rhythm, effort, fatigue and how the
whole body could organize physical work more efficiently.

Their memorable slogan was: 'To bring that swing and lilt in labour, which makes efficiency a pleasure.'
ON SCREEN: Display the quotation as text. Keep attribution in small type: Laban-Lawrence Industrial Rhythm.

Think about that transformation. A movement system developed inside modern dance had now become a way
of analyzing industrial labor. The body was no longer only something that performed. It was something
that could be observed, classified and optimized.
