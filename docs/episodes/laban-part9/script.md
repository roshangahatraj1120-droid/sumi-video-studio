# Laban — Part 9 script: rehabilitation, therapy and Choreometrics

But movement analysis did not only move toward control and efficiency. It also moved toward
rehabilitation, therapy and cultural preservation.

Irmgard Bartenieff, who had trained in the Laban tradition, became a physical therapist. She described
using Laban's movement principles while rehabilitating polio patients. Her work became foundational to
what is now called the Laban/Bartenieff approach.

B-ROLL: Licensed rehabilitation/movement therapy imagery; hands regaining range of motion; warm light.

Research on Laban's wider legacy also traces movement-based work into therapeutic settings involving
people living with trauma, including Holocaust survivors and war veterans.

At the same time, folklorist Alan Lomax collaborated with Bartenieff and Forrestine Paulay on
Choreometrics. Their goal was enormous: study movement patterns across cultures and investigate what
dance could reveal about social organization and human culture.
