# Short-form reels: clip report

There are 12 reels in total, 4 from each documentary. All 12 are rendered as 1080×1920 MP4 files (H.264 video, AAC audio). The subtitles are synced word by word to the actual speech.

**Source timestamps:** these come from the full-length videos. The silent gaps inside each range were removed, which is why each reel is shorter than its source range.

**Subtitles:** I transcribed the speech automatically, then corrected names and terms by hand (Herc, Natya Shastra, Nataraja, Labanotation, Hewlett-Packard and so on). The meaning was not changed. Please watch each reel once before posting to check the subtitles.

**Format of every reel:**
- **Top:** the series label and the hook title. The hook is big for the first 2.5 seconds, then shrinks into a header.
- **When you're on camera:** a full-height vertical crop centred on you.
- **B-roll:** shown whole (so its baked-in titles aren't cropped) over a blurred fill.
- **Captions and audio:** bold captions with the current word highlighted in gold. The audio is cleaned up and levelled to −14 LUFS.
- **Ending:** the last 2 seconds show a "▶ Full documentary on YouTube" tag.
