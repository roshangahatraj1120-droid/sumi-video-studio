# Laban — Part 5 script: 1936 and exile

In 1936, Laban was preparing a huge work for the cultural program surrounding the Berlin Olympics.
Its title was Vom Tauwind und der Neuen Freude - From the Spring Wind and the New Joy.
More than a thousand dancers were involved.

Then Joseph Goebbels attended a rehearsal. The work was rejected. The production was cancelled.
And Laban's position collapsed.

After falling from favor, Laban left Germany. He spent time in Paris and eventually reached England
with help from people including his former student Kurt Jooss and the Dartington network.
His German career was effectively over.

But his ideas about movement were only beginning their second life.
