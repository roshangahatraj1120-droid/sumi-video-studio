# Laban — Part 4 "Politics" · CapCut placement guide

All clips: 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 CRF 10 with synthesised SFX, text baked in. One clip per script
beat, in order. Drop each on V2 over its line and trim the tail to your voiceover.

**Photos only.** Every shot is one of your photos with still movement: a slow push, pull or drift, and
in clip 09 a speed-ramped pull-back. On top of that are split screens, torn-paper cuts, a whip pan and
keyword text. No drawn graphics. No images were sent for this section, so it reuses 01, 02, 03, 05, 07,
16 and 18 from Part 1 and the portrait from Part 2. **No Nazi insignia appear anywhere.**
