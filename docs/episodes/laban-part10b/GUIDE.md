Covers **"A system designed partly to preserve something beautiful also demonstrated…"**. Place it in
Part 10 **right before clip 08's split screen** ("…that movement could be measured, classified and used").
Trim clip 08's opening portrait so the split lands on "measured". 1920×1080, 30 fps, 9.0s, CRF 10.

| Time | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| 0–3.7s | The 1920s dancer mid-leap in warm window light, on her own depth layer so she floats in front of the studio as the camera slowly pushes in; a light sweep crosses her. *a system designed partly to preserve* ***something beautiful*** (gold). |

As the line passes each joint, a **tracking marker snaps onto it** (hand, elbow, shoulder, head, hip, knee, foot) and the skeleton lines join up. One hand is marked in red. |
| 5.9–9.0s | She is now fully tracked. Small readouts type on (**R.ARM ↗ 12° · L.ARM ↑ 81° · LEG → 0.4s**): ***also demonstrated…*** |
