# Follow cards (Instagram + Facebook) · CapCut guide

Two 6-second lower-third cards for your on-camera moments in the middle of the video.
1920×1080, 30 fps, no sound baked in.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `follow-instagram_ALPHA.mov` / `follow-facebook_ALPHA.mov` | **Best.** Transparent background: drop it on a track above your footage. Nothing to remove. |
| `follow-instagram_GREEN.mp4` / `follow-facebook_GREEN.mp4` | Backup if the .mov won't import: put it above your footage → **Cutout → Chroma key**, pick the green, Strength about 20. |
| `…_PREVIEW.mp4` | Just to watch how it looks. Don't put it in the edit. |

## What happens (6.0s)
| Time | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0.0–0.7s | Your photo pops in and the ring draws round it (Instagram gradient / Facebook blue). |
| 0.4–1.2s | The panel slides out: **FOLLOW THE DANCER**, *Roshan Gahatraj*, and your handle typing on. |
| 1.2s | *Dancer · Choreographer · World of Dance Nepal* and the **Follow** button appear. |
| 2.8s | The button is tapped, ripples, and flips to **Following ✓**. |
| 3.3–4.3s | A soft light sweep crosses the card. |
| 4.9–6.0s | The card folds back into your photo, which shrinks away. |

## Where to put them
- **Instagram:** about **5:10**, as you start *"Across the Atlantic, another branch of Laban's system was developing."*
- **Facebook:** about **6:20**, as you start *"Then Laban's ideas moved even farther away from dance."*

Only over shots where **you** are on camera, with the bottom-left corner clear. If your framing puts you
on the left, move the card in CapCut (**Basic → Position**, X about +1000) to put it bottom-right.

## Optional sound
One soft `click` or `pop` at **2.8s** (the tap), 20%. Nothing else.
