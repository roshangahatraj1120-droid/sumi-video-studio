# Hip-hop episode — SFX sheet for CapCut (start to finish)

**Time** = seconds from the start of that clip. Put all SFX on **one audio track** under your voice.
Rule: sound only where the picture hits; long stretches have nothing. 45 cues in about 10 minutes.

## Your sound kit: find once, reuse the same file every time
| Code | CapCut search | Pick one that is… | Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| **KICK** | `kick drum` | one dry, distant 70s kick | 45% |
| **WHOOSH** | `whoosh` | fast, airy, about 0.5s | 35% |
| **IMPACT** | `cinematic impact` | low, soft, short tail | 40% |
| **BOOM** | `cinematic boom` | deep, long tail | 50% |
| **GLITCH** | `glitch` | short, digital | 25% |
| **REWIND** | `vhs rewind` or `tape rewind` | short, 0.5–1s | 30% |
| **STAMP** | `stamp` | rubber-stamp thump | 30% |
| **TAPE** | `tape` | masking tape pulled or stuck | 15% |
| **VINYL** | `record scratch` or `vinyl` | one short scratch | 25% |
| **STOP** | `record stop` or `tape stop` | music grinding to a halt | 35% |

One-offs: `typewriter`, `fire whoosh`, `spray can`, `camera focus` (or `beep`), `light switch`,
`paper rip`, `mouse click`.

## Rules
- **The silences matter most:** the cold open (only the kick), after "it is gone", after "STOP.", the 2s
  after "?" in Chapter 7, and the black after "A move." Don't put anything in them.
- **No SFX under you talking on camera**, and none on the cuts not listed here.
- **Always the same WHOOSH, IMPACT and BOOM.** Repetition is what makes it sound designed.
- **Turn off the placeholder sound inside every clip** (select clip → Volume 0), since all SFX come from this sheet.
- **Monetization:** keep CapCut's *Commercial use* filter on, or take the same search words to the
  YouTube Audio Library → Sound effects.
