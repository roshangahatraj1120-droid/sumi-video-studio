Script line (Ch2): *"And one of the DJs who became famous for understanding that reaction was a Jamaican-born teenager named Clive Campbell."* [ON SCREEN: CLIVE CAMPBELL / DJ KOOL HERC]

These three clips play back to back (about 17s in total). Trim the ends to match your VO. They can replace the older `c2-08-clive-campbell` or sit alongside it.

| 3 | c2-08c-record-label-name.mp4 | 5.5s | "named Clive Campbell." | A record spins down with motion blur and stops dead with CLIVE CAMPBELL upright on the red label. Frame shake and flash, then the name slams in. "DJ KOOL HERC" is stamped in red, with a light sweep. |

No face is tied to his name: the name is typography only, and the photos are anonymous DJs. Every clip carries the VISUAL RECONSTRUCTION tag.
