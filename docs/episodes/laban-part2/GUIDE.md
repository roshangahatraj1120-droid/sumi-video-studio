## Overlays for host-on-camera shots (`overlays/`)
The five-concepts cards from clip 04 with no background, so you can use them over yourself instead:
- `04-concepts-left_*` — cards on the left third (stand frame-right).
- `04-concepts-right_*` — cards on the right third (stand frame-left).
- `_ALPHA.mov` is transparent ProRes 4444 (best). `_GREEN.mp4` is chroma-green — use CapCut **Chroma key**.
