# Laban documentary — minimal SFX cue sheet (CapCut)

**Rule:** sound only on the big moments: 26 cues across the whole video, 2–3 per chapter.
Everything else is voice and music.

## How to add them in CapCut (desktop)
1. Top-left **Audio** tab → **Sound effects** → type the search word from the table.
2. Click a result to preview it, then drag it onto **one separate audio track** under your voice.
3. Put the playhead on the first frame of the clip, move forward by the **Time in clip**, and drop the
   sound there. For impacts, line up the start of the sound's peak with that frame.
4. **Monetization:** if CapCut shows a **"Commercial use"** filter or label on sound effects, keep it on
   and only pick sounds marked for it. If you're unsure about a sound, take the same search word to
   **YouTube Studio → Audio Library → Sound effects** instead (always safe).

## Volume (select the sound → Basic → Volume)
| Type | Volume | Fade |
|---|---|---|
| Whoosh / swoosh | 30–40% | none |
| Impact / hit | 40% | 0.2s fade-out |
| Low boom | 50% | 1s fade-out |
| Glitch | 30% | none |
| Ticks, typewriter, shutter, stamp | 25–35% | none |
| Ambience (train, projector, wind) | 15–20% | 1s fade in and out |

Never louder than your voice.

## Leave out
- No SFX under the host talking.
- No sound on every cut or every word.
- No whooshes on Part 3's chain of whip pans; music alone works better there.
- Don't pick a different whoosh every time. Reuse **the same 2 whooshes, 1 impact and 1 boom** all
  video long so it sounds designed, not random.
