## 2. The look: what makes it
1. **35 mm film-strip frame.** The whole video plays inside a vertical film strip.
   - Black strip borders about 70–80 px wide on each side of 720.
   - Sprocket holes on both edges. Each hole is a small rounded rectangle with an orange/amber glow, spaced about 50 px apart.
   - Faint orange edge-code printing runs vertically along the strip ("KODAK 5063"-style text).
   - The picture sits in a centre "frame" (about 580 px wide) with rounded corners.
   - The **same shot is duplicated** in a partial frame above and below, separated by dark gaps of about 40–60 px, so it reads as a contact strip.
2. **Strip advance between shots.** At each cut, the strip slides vertically with heavy motion blur, like film advancing through a gate (2.25 s, 6.25 s, 14.75 s and so on). The new shot settles into the gate within 3–5 frames.
3. **Light leaks on the beat.** A full-frame red, pink or magenta wash flashes for 2–4 frames roughly every 3 s: at 4.25, 7.25, 13.5, 16.5, 19.5 and 22.5 s. There's one big white lens-flare bloom at 10.0–10.75 s, the midpoint.
4. **Red edge-burn line.** A thin vertical red light line flashes at the right edge: 8.75, 11.75, 15.25, 18.0, 21.0 and 24.0 s. That's also about every 3 s, offset from the leaks.
5. **Stutter cut** (14.4–15.0 s, `stutter-cut.jpg`): image, 2 black frames, image, 3 black frames, the new shot (once), 1 black frame, the new shot settles. This is the "projector skipping" moment.
6. **Viewfinder overlay** (5.5–5.75 s): a circular lens or rangefinder ring with curved "ZOOM LENS" text and a centre square briefly overlays a transition.
7. **Grade: warm teal-orange vintage.**
   - **Shadows** are near-black with a red-brown tint (about RGB 8, 3, 2).
   - **Mid-tones** are pushed orange (about 82, 43, 32).
   - **Highlights** are cool and blue-ish (about 135, 133, 145), which gives a split-tone look.
   - **Saturation** is high in the oranges and reds (0.65–0.74) and lower in neutral shots (about 0.5).
   - **Texture:** slight haze or bloom, soft focus edges, white dust specks and small scratches.
8. **Camera:** every shot is a slow, steady handheld drift (push-in, slide or tilt) on a close detail, with shallow depth of field. No people, no fast moves.

**Rhythm:** a new shot or visual event every **2.5–3.3 s**, never faster except for the stutter. There are about 8 main shots in 24 s.

## 4. Sound
- **0–2.8 s: projector sound effects.** Dense, bright transients with high frequencies (centroid about 4.5 kHz), the mechanical clatter of a film projector starting up. That's the audio hook.
- **2.8 s onward: vintage, lo-fi instrumental** with little bass and a warm mid-range. The main tempo is about **73 BPM** (about 146 counted in double time).
- **Accent hits** come in small clusters every **about 3.3 s**: 5.8–6.2, 9.9–10.2, 12.9–13.1, 16.0–16.4 and 19.6–19.9 s. **The light leaks, red lines and cuts land on or just after these accents.** That's why the edit feels musical even though it's slow.
- The track itself couldn't be identified from audio alone. For your version, use any licensed vintage, jazz or lo-fi track around 70–75 BPM with a clear phrase every 2 bars, plus a projector-start sound effect.

## 5. Recipe: rebuilding it (Remotion or CapCut)
1. **Footage:** 8–10 slow detail shots of 3–4 s each. Shoot in 4K or 1080p, vertical or cropped centre, on a gimbal or slow handheld, with shallow depth of field. Avoid people's faces in close-up. The style is "objects that hold memory".
2. **Open:** a projector-beam clip or a light-flare plate, with a 1.7 s white bloom into the first frame.
3. **Film-strip overlay** (a PNG or Remotion component):
   - The strip and sprocket holes (orange glow).
   - Edge text.
   - A 580×580 rounded gate.
   - Duplicate partial frames above and below, at 75% brightness.
4. **Strip advance** at each cut: translate Y by one frame-pitch over 4–5 frames, with vertical motion blur at 0.6 shutter.
5. **Leaks:** a red or pink full-frame wash in screen blend mode, at 60–80% for 3 frames, then fading out over 4 frames, on every musical accent (about every 3.3 s). A thin red edge-burn line at the right edge, offset by 1.5 s.
6. **One big flare** at the midpoint, and **one stutter cut** at about 60% of the runtime.
7. **Grade:** lift the blacks slightly, then warm them. Push the mids orange and cool the highlights (split-tone). Add +15% orange saturation, light halation and bloom, fine grain and dust specks.
8. **Outro:** a logo badge bottom-right for the last 2 s. No other text, because the visuals carry it.
9. **Audio:** projector start sound under the open, then the music at −14 LUFS.
